For the reported and question rows a *lower* number is better: describing or asking about a view should pass. In multi-clause items the attribution covers only the first clause, and the later, unattributed clauses ("...; he never lived.") are rightly read as the speaker's own assertion; the single-clause rows isolate the framing itself.
